## Basic Operations

### Adding a Manufacturer to a Product
1. Open a product
2. Go to the "Manufacturers" tab
3. Click "Add a line"
4. Fill in:
   - Manufacturer
   - Manufacturer Product Name
   - Manufacturer Code
   - Product URL

### Linking a Supplier with Manufacturer
1. Open product supplier information
2. Select the corresponding manufacturer information
3. The System will automatically link the information

### Searching Products by Manufacturer
- Use filters in the products view
- Search by manufacturer name or manufacturer code

## Tips
- Keep manufacturer codes consistent for better tracking
- Use meaningful manufacturer product names
- Regularly update manufacturer URLs to ensure validity
